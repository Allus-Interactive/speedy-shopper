# Speedy Shopper

Speedy Shopper is a small 3D game where you pick and deliver customers shopping orders built in Godot

<b>Genre:</b> Walking/Driving Simulator

<b>Tech Stack:</b> Godot 4.7. Blender. Affinity Designer.

<b>Platform(s):</b> Windows

<b>Prototype Date:</b> 3rd October 2026 at the [IAM Gaming Expo](https://www.indiegamesquad.co.uk/iam-gaming-expo/)

<b>Demo Launch Date:</b> Q4 2026

<b>Anticipated Full Launch date:</b> Q2 2027

<b>Launch Platforms:</b> itch.io (Demo and Full Launch), Steam (Full Launch)

<b>Brief Game Summary:</b> Speedy Shopper is a first person walking/driving simulator. The game revolves around the player's job as a personal shopper/delivery driver. Orders will be sent in to your PDA, which need to be accepted and picked from your shop. Once a set of orders have been picked, you need to then deliver them in your van. Every delivery will reward you with a percentage of the order delivered. An order cannot be delivered until it is fully picked.
